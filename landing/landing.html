About Me

Technology does not exist in a vacuum. It exists in communities, businesses, and in the hands of people trying to get things done. That is the lens I bring to every system I design.

As a Zimbabwean developer, I think carefully about the user of software and their contexts. I design for people who may not have reliable internet connection or the latest hardware. Technology serving African communities must be dependable, practical, and impactful.

My personal commitment is to build systems that are better, safer, and relevant to the times. To ensure this, I actively pursue practical courses where building real solutions is part of the learning process. This allows me to continue expanding my technical expertise as technology evolves.

Many people think Computer Science is about writing code. I believe it's about solving problems for the people who rely on the systems we build.

If you're looking for a developer who bridges technical expertise with practical solutions. Someone who creates value for people and communities. Then look no further and let's get in touch.
